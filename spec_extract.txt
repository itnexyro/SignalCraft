The platform adopts a high-precision, developer-grade aesthetic (inspired by Vercel and Linear) emphasizing dark/light mode

(#09090b) with blue accent (#3b82f6).

AI Cold Outreach Agent • Data Protection, Security Standards & Terms of Service
1. Privacy Policy Summary
Data Collection & Security
We collect user account information, uploaded spreadsheet datasets, and encrypted Gmail App Passwords solely for the
execution of outreach campaigns. Zero Data Sharing: Uploaded spreadsheets and generated copy are never sold, rented, or
utilized to train third-party public LLM models. All credentials are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM.
2. Terms of Service Summary
Acceptable Use & Compliance
Users agree to use the AI Cold Outreach Agent in strict compliance with anti-spam regulations (including CAN-SPAM and
GDPR where applicable). Users are solely responsible for ensuring recipient consent. Account Suspension: The platform
reserves the right to terminate accounts engaging in abusive spam practices or exceeding safe Gmail rate limits.
3. Compliance & Operational Guardrails
Rate Throttling: Enforces mandatory delays between dispatches to protect sender reputation.
Data Retention: Campaign records and recipient data can be purged permanently by the user at any time from dashboard
settings.
